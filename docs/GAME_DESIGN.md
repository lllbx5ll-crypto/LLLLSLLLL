# مدينة التجار / Merchants City — Game Design

**Developer credit:** Al-Qmishi

## Core progression
1. Worker: talk to employer, complete paid shift; work has cooldown.
2. Market seller: earn 250 coins, buy a stall, purchase stock from supplier and set competitive prices.
3. Shop owner: earn 1,750, buy a shop; rename its Arabic sign and hire workers.
4. Business owner: maintain inventory, respond to price fluctuations, deliveries, wages and rent.

## Android controls
On-screen directional buttons for walking; action buttons to work, buy, sell, buy stall/shop, hire worker, name store, adjust prices, and save. Desktop arrows/WASD and E for context action.

## Current content / limitations
Procedurally generated low-poly city and inhabitants, 12 fictional realistic product types (not copied brands), original synthesized placeholder sound effects, day/night, save/load, 3D camera and Arabic labels. This is not yet photorealistic; detailed licensed models, realistic voices, traffic systems, extensive interiors and multiplayer are future production features.

## Principles
Respect image/model/music rights. Never bundle copyrighted commercial brands or celebrity likenesses without permission. Optimize for Android memory, draw calls and CPU. All in-game currency is fictional.
